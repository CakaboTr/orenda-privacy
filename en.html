1. Introduction

Orenda is a mystical-content app developed by LAKADOKI. It offers coffee-cup readings, tarot, dream interpretation, birth charts, numerology, and other traditional symbolic readings. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. Orenda is for entertainment purposes only — it does not provide real predictions or medical/psychological advice.

2. Data We Collect

👤 Personal Info (You Provide)

  • Name — used for personalized greetings during onboarding
  • Date of birth — used for zodiac and numerology calculations
  • Birth time — optional, used for natal-chart calculations
  • Gender — optional, used to personalize wording
  • Birth place — optional, used for natal-chart calculations
  • Relationship and work status — optional, used to fit readings to your life

This information is stored only on your device; we do not store it on any server. When you ask for a reading, it is sent to Google Gemini to prepare that reading (see Section 4).

📷 Photos and Texts

  • Coffee-cup / palm / tea-cup photos — sent to Google Gemini API for reading generation, not stored on our servers
  • Dream descriptions — sent to Gemini for interpretation, not stored on our servers
  • Questions and names you write (e.g. partner-name compatibility) — sent to Gemini for reading generation, not stored on our servers

In the iPhone app they are kept only on your device, so that your reading history can reopen them.

📱 App Data

  • Device language — for UI language selection
  • App preferences (theme, notification toggles, etc.)
  • Reading history — only on your device
  • Credit and streak data — only on your device
  • Advertising data — for AdMob: an anonymous Advertising ID on Android; the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used on iPhone (see Section 4)

3. How We Use the Data

  • To produce personalized mystical content (zodiac, numerology, readings)
  • To generate AI-powered interpretations via Google Gemini API
  • To show ads and manage subscriptions
  • To improve the app and fix bugs

4. Third-Party Services

🤖 Google Gemini API

For AI interpretations, your profile details (name, zodiac sign, birth date, time and place, gender, relationship and work status), your photos and what you write are sent to Google's Gemini service through Firebase. The iPhone app asks for your explicit permission before the first reading; if you do not allow it, nothing is sent, and you can withdraw your permission at any time in the app's Settings. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

📺 Google AdMob

Ads are served by Google AdMob. In the Android app, an anonymous Advertising ID and IP address are shared with the ad service; you can reset your ad consent any time from Profile → Ad Consent.

The iPhone app only shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch. It does not ask for tracking permission (ATT) and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). For ads, the device ID, advertising data, coarse location, product interaction, and crash, performance and other diagnostic data reach Google; none of it is used for tracking. You can change your ad consent in Settings → Ad consent; this row appears only in regions where consent is required.

🛡️ Firebase App Check

To verify that AI requests come from the genuine, unmodified Orenda app, a device attestation token (Apple App Attest on iPhone, Google Play Integrity on Android) is sent to Google Firebase.

💎 Google Play and App Store

Premium subscription purchases are handled by Google Play Billing on Android and by the App Store on iPhone. Payment data is managed entirely by Google or Apple; Orenda does not access your payment details.

5. Data Retention

All personal data, reading history, and in-app data are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data via system settings will erase all of it. We do not keep any personal data on a server.

Photos and texts sent to the Gemini API are processed by Google; please review Google's retention policies via the link above.

6. Cookie Policy

The Orenda mobile app does not use cookies.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know whether your personal data is being processed
  • Know what data is being held
  • Request deletion or destruction of your data
  • Request correction of incorrect or incomplete data
  • Erase all your data by uninstalling the app

Send your requests to lakadokistore@gmail.com.

8. GDPR — European Union Users

If you are a resident of the European Union, GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) grants you equivalent rights: access, rectification, erasure, portability, and the right to object. In-app ad consent is managed via UMP (User Messaging Platform); you can withdraw consent at any time.

9. CCPA — California Users

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion. Send your request to lakadokistore@gmail.com.

10. Children's Privacy

Orenda is designed for users 13 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we become aware that data of a child under 13 has been processed, we will delete it immediately.

11. Disclaimer

Orenda is an entertainment app. Its content:

  • Should not be interpreted as medical, psychological, or financial advice
  • Should not be used as the sole basis for any decision
